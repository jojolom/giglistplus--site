Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 6, 2026

GigList+ ("the App") is developed by Joe Lombardi. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

GigList+ does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. The App does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks.

Data Storage

All data you create in the App — including songs, setlists, and page images — is stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service. This data is:

  • Stored in your private iCloud container, accessible only to you
  • Synced across your devices signed into the same Apple ID
  • Not accessible to the developer or any third party

iCloud Sync

If you are signed into iCloud on your device, the App uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your data across your devices. This sync is managed entirely by Apple's infrastructure and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. You can disable iCloud sync for the App in your device's Settings.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs. No data is shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children. The App does not collect information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at the support page.
