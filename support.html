GigList+ Help & FAQ

Getting Started

GigList+ helps you organize your songs and build setlists for performances. Add songs to your Song Bank, attach page images (sheet music, chord charts, lyrics), then build setlists by picking songs from your bank.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a song?

Go to the Song Bank tab and tap the + button. Enter the song title and add page images from your photo library or camera.

How do I create a setlist?

Go to the Setlists tab and tap the + button. Name your setlist, then tap "Add Songs" to pick songs from your Song Bank.

How does Performance Mode work?

Open a setlist and tap the play button. Performance Mode displays your song pages full-screen. Swipe left and right to navigate. The screen stays on while performing. Tap the X button to exit.

Does my data sync across devices?

Yes. If you're signed into iCloud, your songs and setlists automatically sync across all your devices using the same Apple ID.
